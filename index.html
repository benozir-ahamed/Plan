The Challenge

Generic Marketing Doesn't Work

Businesses struggle to identify valuable customers and engage them effectively. Traditional marketing is generic, time-consuming, and fails to deliver personalized content, leading to low engagement and lost revenue. Managing separate tools for analysis, content generation, and execution only adds complexity.

The Solution

One Integrated AI Platform

We combine data science, generative AI, and marketing automation into a single, seamless platform. Automatically segment your customers, generate tailored email and WhatsApp copy, and execute campaigns to save time, improve engagement, and boost conversions.

Core Features

Everything you need to automate and personalize your marketing, all in one place.

CLV & RFM Segmentation

Automatically analyze customer transaction data to calculate Customer Lifetime Value and segment them into High, Mid, and Low Value groups.

AI-Driven Content Generation

Generative AI creates highly personalized and effective email and WhatsApp copy tailored to each customer segment, increasing engagement.

Seamless Email Automation

Connect your Mailchimp account to automatically send the generated emails to the correct customer segments, eliminating manual effort.

Direct WhatsApp Engagement

Connect with customers directly on WhatsApp. Our AI generates unique messages for each segment. Boost engagement with interactive buttons and quick replies, and track campaign results—all from one platform.
